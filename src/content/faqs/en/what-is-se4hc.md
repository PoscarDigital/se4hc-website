The Secondary Education for Human Capital Competitiveness (SE4HC) Project is an $83.92 million initiative funded by the Asian Development Bank and the Government of Cambodia to strengthen Cambodia's secondary education system. The project aims to improve access to quality upper secondary education, enhance alignment with 21st-century skills, and strengthen post-secondary education pathways.
