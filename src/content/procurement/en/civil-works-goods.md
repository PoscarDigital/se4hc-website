Civil works and goods contracts are currently at the procurement planning stage and will be tendered through Open Competitive Bidding (OCB). These contracts will cover the construction and equipping of 414 classrooms, 140 science laboratories, and 111 computer labs across project schools, with opportunities to be published once planning is finalized.
