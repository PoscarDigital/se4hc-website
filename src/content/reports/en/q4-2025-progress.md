The **Quarterly Progress Report Q4 2025** provides a comprehensive overview of project implementation during October–December 2025.

## Key Highlights

- **Physical progress:** 6.0% (against 13.0% elapsed time)
- **School site assessments:** 245 schools assessed
- **Fast-track pilot:** Program initiated at 25 schools
- **Contract awards:** $901,264 across 13 contracts
- **Disbursement:** $1,163,900 (1.45%)

The report also covers the development of CPD materials for STEM teachers and progress on procurement planning for civil works and goods.
