The **ADB Review Mission Report — October 2025** presents the findings and recommendations of the Asian Development Bank's review mission, assessing the project's overall implementation and outlining agreed actions for the coming quarter.

## Mission Findings

- **Implementation status** reviewed against the project administration manual and targets
- **Procurement progress** evaluated for civil works and goods packages
- **Safeguards compliance** assessed for environmental and social requirements
- **Action items** agreed for follow-up in the next quarter

The mission confirmed satisfactory progress and provided guidance to strengthen implementation momentum.
