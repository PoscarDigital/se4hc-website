The **Project Administration Manual (PAM)** is the official reference document that governs how the SE4HC project is implemented, providing detailed guidance for all executing and implementing agencies.

## Contents

- **Implementation guidelines** defining roles, responsibilities, and arrangements
- **Procurement procedures** for civil works, goods, and consulting services
- **Financial management requirements** covering disbursement, accounting, and audit
- **Safeguards framework** for environmental and social compliance

The PAM serves as the primary operational manual throughout the project life cycle.
