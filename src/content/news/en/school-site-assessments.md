The SE4HC project has completed comprehensive site assessments at 245 secondary schools across 25 provinces in Cambodia. These assessments evaluated existing facilities, land availability, and site-specific conditions to inform the design and construction of new and upgraded learning environments.

The assessments enable the construction of 414 classrooms, along with science labs, computer labs, and WASH (water, sanitation, and hygiene) facilities. This milestone forms a key part of the project's fast-track approach, accelerating the delivery of improved infrastructure to schools and communities nationwide.
