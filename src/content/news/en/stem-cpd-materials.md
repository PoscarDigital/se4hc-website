The SE4HC project has developed comprehensive Continuous Professional Development (CPD) materials for Science and Mathematics teachers. The materials focus on STEM education methodologies, project-based learning, and 21st-century teaching skills, equipping educators to deliver more engaging and effective instruction.

Designed to reach more than 3,000 teachers, the CPD package supports the project's goal of strengthening the quality of science and mathematics education across participating secondary schools.
