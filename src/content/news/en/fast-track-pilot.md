The SE4HC project has launched its fast-track pilot program at 25 selected schools. The pilot delivers accelerated infrastructure improvements alongside targeted teacher training, allowing the project team to demonstrate early impact at the school level.

Lessons learned from the pilot will be used to refine implementation approaches and inform the wider rollout of activities across the project's participating schools and provinces.
