This comprehensive curriculum supports the continuous professional development of Science and Mathematics teachers under the SE4HC project. It provides structured modules, lesson exemplars, and hands-on activities designed to strengthen STEM pedagogy and improve learning outcomes in secondary schools across Cambodia.
